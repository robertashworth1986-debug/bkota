01 / Lettering

Words you can actually read.

The verse is real text inside the vector artwork—not lettering baked into a generated photograph. The enlarged view lets you check every word.

02 / Resolution

Sharpness without a pixel ceiling.

SVG paths and text scale cleanly. The source uses a 4000 × 5000 coordinate grid with a 12 × 15 inch artboard; it is not a claim of “4K” fabric or a finished production proof.

03 / Production

A printer’s proof comes first.

Type remains editable and depends on installed Arial/Helvetica or Georgia/Times fonts. Your printer should verify fonts or convert them to outlines, confirm garment size, ink colors, underbase, placement, and a physical sample before manufacture.
