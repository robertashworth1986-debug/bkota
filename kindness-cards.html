Arthur's take-five challenge

Carry a word of kindness.

Launch proof: BKOTA.co currently opens a password-protected storefront. Review and sample-print these cards, but do not distribute them until Arthur’s public movement page is live at the printed address.

Print, cut, and carry these cards. Offer them freely to a neighbor during ordinary life. Never pressure anyone, interrupt workers, place cards on vehicles, or leave them where permission is required. A gracious “no thank you” is always respected.
