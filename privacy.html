Privacy & community safety

A safe place for kindness.

Current status: This public BKOTA site is a static community and merchandise showcase. A shared submission wall, online reporting service, and payments are not connected. Stories and video links entered into the private preview are not sent to Arthur or published for other visitors.

Shared preview protection: On the temporary robertashworth1986-debug.github.io address, BKOTA does not write new stories or video links to browser storage. They last only while that particular page instance remains available. This prevents BKOTA's personal preview entries from being placed in browser storage shared with unrelated GitHub Pages projects. Download anything you want to keep. If you used an older BKOTA preview, its earlier browser copy may remain until you clear site data for that GitHub Pages address.

Community safeguards

  • Ask everyone identifiable for permission before filming and before posting. Receiving help must never depend on agreeing to be filmed.
  • Respect a refusal or a later request to remove a post. A good deed counts even when it is never recorded.
  • Do not record private, unsafe, embarrassing, or vulnerable moments. Avoid addresses, school names, medical details, and other identifying information.
  • BKOTA's initial public review queue will not accept videos that identify a child or capture anyone in a medical, crisis, private, humiliating, or otherwise vulnerable moment.
  • A future shared wall must have a verified moderation and reporting service before public submissions are enabled. A public video link does not establish permission or ownership; the submitter must control the posting account or have its owner's authorization.

Report or request removal

Online reports are currently unavailable. Use the Clear controls on the home page to remove entries from this browser's private preview. Clearing a local preview does not delete the original video from YouTube or TikTok. If a video violates your privacy, contact the person who posted it or use that platform's reporting tools.

The form below remains disabled unless an explicitly connected service confirms it can accept privacy reports. A receipt will confirm that a request was received, not that a post was removed or that a moderator has decided the case. Do not enter sensitive information into this unavailable form.

Storage and measurement

This site's application-level impact measurement is disabled. It does not load advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. Hosting providers still receive ordinary network requests when you visit and may keep logs; this page does not claim that the host keeps no connection information.

On the dedicated bkota.co domain, the site may store private preview entries and your background-motion preference on this device. On the shared GitHub preview, only the non-sensitive motion preference may persist; stories and video links are not written to browser storage. If browser storage is blocked or full, a persistent entry cannot be saved: keep the form open and copy your text somewhere safe before leaving. Private entries are not a backup. Do not store confidential information in a preview.

A service worker can cache public pages, styles, scripts, and artwork for repeat visits. It does not cache configuration, API requests, or private report submissions. Browser storage and cached pages can be removed through your browser's site-data settings.

Before any shared service is launched, its actual hosting, access controls, contact route, retention periods, deletion process, and moderation coverage must be verified and this notice updated. This static release does not promise unverified server-side deletion schedules or privacy protections.

Payments

No payment or donation flow is active. A Venmo destination will be shown only after Arthur approves the exact recipient. BKOTA will not ask for card numbers or passwords on this website.

Social platforms

BKOTA does not automatically load YouTube or TikTok players, thumbnail images, or scripts. Choosing a video link opens the provider's site, where its terms and privacy practices apply. A link in your private preview is not an approved or independently verified BKOTA story. Native sharing and copying an invitation are actions you choose; BKOTA does not automatically post to your social accounts.
