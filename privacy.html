Privacy & community safety

A safe place for kindness.

Current status: BKOTA supports two clearly labeled modes. With the moderated service connected, consented stories and public social-video links enter a private review queue and remain unpublished until a human approves them. When that service is unavailable, entries remain only in that browser's local storage.

Community safeguards

  • Every submission requires clear consent before it is accepted.
  • Stories and videos remain pending until a human moderator approves them.
  • Do not record someone in a private, unsafe, or vulnerable moment.
  • Content involving children requires heightened consent and safety review before any publication.
  • Anyone shown in a story can submit the private review request below.
  • BKOTA collects only the information needed to operate and protect the movement.
  • Resolving a linked removal request unpublishes that record and clears the requester's contact details; a trusted helper can permanently delete records.

Report or request removal

If you appear in a BKOTA story or linked video and want it reviewed, use the review link beside that item and explain the concern. The item is placed on hold immediately while the private request waits for a human moderator. Contact information is optional; without it, BKOTA cannot send a reply.

Choose “Request privacy or removal review” beside the BKOTA item first.

Anonymous impact measurement

BKOTA's shared-service measurement is disabled by default. When it is deliberately enabled after the production privacy checks pass, BKOTA counts measured page loads, valid campaign-link loads, uses of its share controls, accepted submissions, and moderation outcomes in daily totals. These totals can include repeat visits and automated traffic. They are not unique visitors, verified shares, people reached, independently verified deeds, or proof of social or political influence.

The measurement feature does not use analytics cookies, device fingerprints, advertising identifiers, raw visit records, stored referrer histories, or stored user-agent histories. A campaign code identifies an approved asset such as a scripture-card print run or billboard—not a person, household, email address, or one-to-one handoff. The clear code is removed from the visible URL after it is read and is stored by the server only as a one-way digest.

Network and application security controls may temporarily process a connection address to limit abuse. That address is not placed in BKOTA's impact tables or audit details. Before measurement is enabled publicly, the deployment checklist requires a review of the effective proxy and provider logs; this page does not claim that an unverified host or CDN keeps no network logs.

On the moderated service, the scheduled database sweep removes daily impact totals after 760 days, provider quota counters after 35 days, closed rejected/removed content and redacted resolved requests after 30 days, and rejected/removed discovery candidates after 90 days. A successful backup run schedules local snapshots older than 30 days for deletion; failed or disabled timers can retain data longer and must be monitored. Pending safety requests are not automatically discarded, and approved public material remains until it is removed or deleted. The hash-chain audit ledger retains opaque record identifiers, action labels, timestamps, and non-content metadata without automatic expiry; it excludes story text, report contact/details, credentials, and clear campaign codes. Campaign labels, asset-code digests, and migration receipts also remain until a documented archival policy is approved. These periods and controls apply only after the timers and backup checks are verified on the actual host.

This browser may use local storage for the background-motion preference and for clearly labeled private preview entries. That functional storage is separate from anonymous impact measurement and is not used to identify a visitor across devices.

Payments

No payment or donation flow is active. A Venmo destination will be shown only after Arthur approves the exact recipient. BKOTA will not ask for card numbers or passwords on this website.

Social platforms

Approved public YouTube and TikTok links may be displayed after moderation. Public BKOTA pages do not automatically load provider frames or scripts; a visitor contacts the platform only after choosing an approved link. In the private administrator console, an intentional YouTube discovery search sends the bounded search text from BKOTA's server to Google's API and may load YouTube thumbnail images, so Google's and YouTube's terms and privacy practices apply to that moderation action. Search text is not stored in BKOTA's audit ledger. Search results enter a separate private candidate queue with rights and consent marked unverified; finding a public video does not publish it or establish reuse rights. API keys are never delivered in browser code.
